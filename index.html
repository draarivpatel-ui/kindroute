How the trace is produced

Match. Capacity is expanded into volunteer slots, then an exact Hungarian solver minimizes a cost matrix built from travel, urgency, skills and deadlines. The six phases you can step through are the solver's own recorded state transitions.

Bundle. Clarke-Wright savings merges nearby stops when load and time budgets stay feasible. Each accepted merge has a positive saving and appears in the route panel.

Polish. 2-Opt reverses segments only when the closed loop gets shorter. Every accepted swap is logged with its exact distance delta; rejected candidates remain in the engine's evaluation trace for inspection.

Compare. The naive baseline walks requests in arrival order and gives each to the first volunteer with capacity. Every number above is computed from a synthetic neighborhood model, not a field trial.

    Zero-knowledge dispatch

    A fresh 256-bit AES-GCM key and a random 96-bit IV are generated for every card. The browser encrypts the JSON route locally and writes ciphertext plus key into the URL fragment. A recipient needs only the link, not an account or a backend.

    Honest limit: the link is the secret. Anyone who can read the fragment can decrypt the card, so share it through a channel you trust. All scenario names and stops here are synthetic.
